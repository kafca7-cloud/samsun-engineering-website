About SAMSUN

35 years on Cat engines, on land and at sea

SAMSUN Engineering Co., Ltd was founded in Yeosu in 1990 and has worked under Caterpillar authorization since 1995, in partnership with HAEIN Corporation, the Cat dealer in Korea. Our technicians are trained to Caterpillar procedures and use Cat diagnostic tools (Cat ET) and S·O·S fluid analysis.

On land we supply, install and maintain Cat diesel generator sets for petrochemical, steel, power and public-sector sites. At sea we service main engines, auxiliary engines and generators on government vessels and commercial ships — and we apply the same procedures to vessels calling at Yeosu and Gwangyang.

Marine Services

Main engines, auxiliary engines and marine generators

Disassembly, overhaul and parts replacement by application area. Each job starts with a diagnosis so the scope is agreed before work begins; the work and the parts replaced are documented for the owner.

Main engine overhaul

Disassembly and overhaul of propulsion engines: measurement of worn components, replacement of consumables, reassembly to Caterpillar specifications and sea trial.

  • Top and major overhaul
  • Cylinder liners, pistons, bearings
  • Sea trial after reassembly

Auxiliary engines

Inspection and repair of auxiliary engines driving pumps, hydraulics and deck machinery. Scope is set from running hours and condition data, not by a fixed menu.

  • Condition-based scope
  • Scheduled maintenance
  • Consumable replacement

Marine generator sets

Scheduled inspection, repair and parts replacement for ship's-service generators. Engine and alternator are checked together.

  • Engine and alternator inspection
  • Preventive maintenance
  • Electrical and control checks

Genuine parts & sourcing

Genuine Cat parts through the Caterpillar dealer network. For discontinued or long-lead items we can review in-house manufacturing of replacement parts.

  • Genuine Cat parts
  • In-house replacement parts (case by case)
  • On-board replacement
How We Work

Diagnosis first, then a scope you can approve

Six steps from diagnosis to handover. Measurements, parts replaced and trial results are recorded and handed over with the vessel.

01

Diagnosis

Running condition and fault codes (Cat ET), S·O·S fluid analysis

02

Disassembly

Open the affected area and inspect internally

03

Measurement

Wear and dimensional checks, parts assessment

04

Repair & parts

Repair or replace according to the agreed scope

05

Reassembly

To Caterpillar specifications, incl. alignment

06

Trial & handover

Sea trial, then the service record

Vessels Calling at Yeosu

Cat-powered vessels in Yeosu and Gwangyang

Foreign-flagged vessels with Caterpillar engines or generators can get Cat-standard service and genuine parts while alongside at Yeosu or Gwangyang, through the Caterpillar dealer network. To let us prepare before arrival, please include the following in your email:

  • Vessel name, flag and agent at Yeosu/Gwangyang
  • Engine model and serial number (engine plate photo is ideal)
  • ETA and berth, and how long the vessel stays
  • The issue: fault codes, symptoms, running hours, last overhaul
